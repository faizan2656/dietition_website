1. About Me

I’m Faryal Tahir, a Dietitian & Nutrition Coach with a BS (Hons) in Human Nutrition & Dietetics.

With over two years of experience in personalised online nutrition coaching, I help clients work towards their health and wellness goals through practical, sustainable, and individualised guidance.

I believe nutrition should fit into your life — not the other way around. My approach focuses on creating realistic nutrition plans around your goals, lifestyle, preferences, and routine, without unnecessary restrictions. My goal is to help you build healthier habits that you can genuinely maintain while still enjoying the foods you love.

Alongside my coaching, I’m a health and nutrition content creator, sharing calorie-counted recipes, balanced meal ideas, and practical nutrition tips to make healthy eating feel simple, enjoyable, and sustainable.
